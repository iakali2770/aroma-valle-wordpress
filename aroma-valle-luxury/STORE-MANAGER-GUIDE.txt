AROMA VALLE' STORE MANAGER - VERSION 2.6.9

WHERE TO MANAGE PRODUCTS
WordPress Dashboard > Aroma Valle' > Products & Prices

ADD A PRODUCT
WordPress Dashboard > Aroma Valle' > Add New Product
Enter the product name, type, collection, brand/inspired-by, fragrance family/notes, customer-facing description, strength, image and bottle prices. Save. Then open Products & Prices to change visibility or stock later.

EDIT AN EXISTING PRODUCT
Open Products & Prices. Select Individual Perfumes or Signature Blends. Update the product fields and prices, then click Save this page of products. Products are shown 15 per page to avoid server input limits.

HIDE / RESTORE A PRODUCT
In Products & Prices, set Website status to Hidden to remove a product from the public shop while retaining its data. Set it back to Active to show it again. This works for both original catalogue products and custom products.

STOCK STATUS
Set Stock status to Out of stock to disable Add to Cart and show an Out of stock message. Orders are also checked server-side. Set back to In stock when available.

DELETE A PRODUCT
Custom products added through Add New Product have a Delete product action. It permanently removes that custom product and its saved overrides. Original catalogue products are not permanently deleted from the bundled catalogue; use Website status = Hidden to safely remove them from the public shop and restore them later.

IMAGES
Use Choose image to select/upload a product photo from the WordPress Media Library. Save the current page after changes.

BACKUP
Before bulk changes, keep a current WordPress backup. Existing catalogue data and product overrides are stored in WordPress options; plugin updates do not overwrite saved overrides.
